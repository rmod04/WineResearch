Wine Tourism

Where the journey begins in the vineyard.

Why Wine Travel?

The best way to understand a wine is to stand in the place it came from.

Wine tourism is not about collecting stamps in a passport or ticking off famous labels. It is about the conversation you have with a winemaker at the end of a long harvest day, the way the light falls across a valley at 6am, the meal that was never on any menu.

The Story

Rohan Modwel is a wine professional, writer, and travel consultant holding a WSET Level 3 Award in Wines with Distinction, with a background in economics, research, and consulting.

His work sits at the intersection of wine, travel, and human connection — spanning immersive travel experiences centred around wine, uncovering data-driven trends with the potential to shape the market, and curating tasting experiences that bring people closer to the story behind the glass.

"Wine should never feel intimidating: at its best, it is a shared language, a catalyst for community, and one of the most honest expressions of place and culture that exists."
