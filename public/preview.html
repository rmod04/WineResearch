Tasting Experiences

An evening built around the bottle.

The best thing about an evening with wine is that it gets better with company, food, and a bit of context. The kind that makes you look at a glass differently and think: I'd like another one of those. Bring an appetite, go back home with a memory.

Tasting Themes

Wine and food is such a vast domain. Here's some ways in which we narrow it down.

🍛

Wine & Indian Cuisine

We all know Indian food is among the most complex in the world. This evening explores what actually works, and why. Spiced dishes and slow cooked meats await.

🗺️

A Journey Through One Region

A single region, explored in depth. Bordeaux, Tuscany, California, Mendoza, Barossa... the list goes on. Many wines, one common story.

🌿

The Language of Terroir

What does a wine taste like when the land it comes from has a distinct character? Soil, altitude, weather systems, water bodies, hills. It all plays a subtle yet critical part.

✍️

Choose Your Own Theme

Every occasion is different. A birthday celebration, a corporate dinner, a curated experience for a specific guest list. We can build the theme around the people and the moment.

Private Dinners

Entirely yours, built for your table.

Whether it's a larger dinner party or a more intimate gathering for guests, we work with the host to choose a theme that suits the occasion, curate the wines and food pairings, and lead the evening at whatever level of depth the table wants, all the way from casual exploration to a more structured guided tasting.

Can be hosted in a public venue or host's residence as per preference. Available for travel to other cities on request.

Planning an evening around wine?

Whether it is a one-off tasting or a recurring supper club format, get in touch and we will build something around your guests and your occasion.

Wine Tourism

The best way to understand a wine is to stand in the place it came from.

The story behind a wine is rarely on the label. It lives in the soil, the climate, and the moment you stand in front of a winemaker who has dedicated a lifetime to a single hillside. It's not what you taste, but what you take back home with you. Of course, you can take some wine too!

Our Partners

Every partner on this page is based on a foundation of taste, experience, and value. Each estate effectively serves as the centre point of a curated itinerary that is designed to fit your style of travel perfectly and provide you with an unforgettable experience.

If you have a specific wine region or estate that you'd like to visit, no problem! Let us know and we'll do all the work behind the scenes.

Bhutan Wine Company

Wine at the edge of the world.

One of the most extraordinary wine stories on earth — a boutique winery nestled in the Himalayas, producing wine at altitude in one of the planet's last great wildernesses.

Signature Wine Experiences

A glimpse into some of the unique moments we can help create.

🧀

Food Pairings

Pair your selection of wines with a cheese board, some nibbles, or if you're a bit more peckish, perhaps a selection of gourmet tapas would do just fine.

🍷

Private Sessions

A one-on-one chat that dives deep into the story and processes behind the labels. Understand the philosophy, taste the wine, pair it with food that speaks the same language.

🪵

Cellar Tastings

Stand in cellars and caves where the wine was born. Private tastings, barrel samples, and the kind of access that no retail shelf can replicate.

🏡

Vineyard Stays

Wake up surrounded by vines. A curated selection of vineyard estates and wine-country hotels where the terroir extends from the glass to the room.

🍇

Harvest Trips

Join a working harvest, get your hands on the fruit, understand what a vintage really means, and share a table with the people who make the wine.
