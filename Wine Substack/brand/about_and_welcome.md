## ABOUT PAGE

**The Deeper Pour is a research-led wine publication from Cork To Table.**

Every piece starts with a question worth asking. Does expensive wine actually taste better? Why does the same bottle feel different in August than it does in January? What happens to a palate after five years of drinking?

Then it goes looking for evidence. Price and rating data across thousands of bottles, blind tasting studies, search behaviour, and the unfiltered conversations wine drinkers have with each other online. The findings are usually more interesting than the received wisdom they test.

Wine should never feel intimidating. At its best it is a shared language, a catalyst for community, and one of the most honest expressions of place and culture that exists. The guiding philosophy of Cork To Table rests on one belief: make wine feel like it belongs to everyone. This publication works from the same belief, with the data doing the talking.

**A note on the charts.** Every article here also lives on corktotable.co, where the charts are fully interactive. Substack cannot run them inside a post, so the versions you see in each piece are static images, and every one links through to the live version on the website.

**Who writes it**

Rohan Modwel is a wine professional, writer and travel consultant based in New Delhi, holding a WSET Level 3 Award in Wines (Distinction) and an MSc in Economics from the London School of Economics. Cork To Table is his wine research, travel and tasting brand.
