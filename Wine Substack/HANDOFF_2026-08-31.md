## Gotchas worth not rediscovering

- **An fswatch watcher on `~/WineResearch-substack` pushes HTML on its own** within a minute or two, so an explicit deploy script often reports "Nothing changed". It stages `visual_*.html` and `findings.html` only. Assets need manual commits.
- **The Cowork sandbox has no egress to cdnjs or Google Fonts.** Rendering visuals for static export requires local asset copies. Do not commit those patched files back as source without checking. This caused a broken-link near-miss on 31 August.
- **Substack cannot render iframes.** Static images plus a link out is the only option.
- **Publication name is free to change, the subdomain is not.** The redirect-preserving subdomain change can be used once.

## Standing rules
