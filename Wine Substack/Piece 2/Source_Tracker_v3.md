## Suggested citation formats

- **Peer-reviewed (P1, P2, P4):** name the journal and year in text, e.g., "in more than 6,000 blind tastings (Goldstein et al., Journal of Wine Economics, 2008)."
- **Industry and market (I1 to I8):** name the organisation and year, e.g., "a Wine Opinions survey found," "Wine Spectator's 2025 Top 100." For I6, add the caveat that it was a brand-commissioned survey.
- **Original data (O1 to O4):** "author's original analysis," e.g., "an analysis of 535 wines on Vivino," "an analysis of 1,626 Google autocomplete suggestions." Attribute quotes to "a wine community member," never a username; include an upvote count only where it adds weight.
